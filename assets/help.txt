regulatory-multiomics-pipeline

Supported assay selectors:
  RNA        short-read RNA-seq, raw or processed counts
  ATAC       paired-end bulk ATAC-seq, raw or processed region counts
  WGBS       conventional paired-end WGBS, raw or processed CpG counts
  H3K27ac    H3K27ac ChIP-seq with explicit matched input control
  INTEGRATION standardized-contract regulatory evidence integration

Essential parameters:
  --assay, --input, --fasta, --gtf, --outdir
  ATAC/H3K27ac additionally require --blacklist for raw and validated processed routes.
  INTEGRATION requires --integration_manifest and an explicit --integration_match_type.

Raw and processed records are mutually exclusive per library. Differential analyses are opt-in,
require an explicit contrast, and retain component-scoped NOT_RUN states when inference is invalid
or disabled. See README.md and docs/ for complete examples and output contracts.
